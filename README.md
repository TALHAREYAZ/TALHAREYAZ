# 👨‍🔬 About Me

> **AI researcher developing intelligent, cooperative and communication-aware autonomous systems for challenging underwater environments.**

I am a **Junior Research Fellow at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore**, working at the intersection of:

- 🧠 **Artificial Intelligence**
- 🤖 **Autonomous Systems**
- 🌊 **Underwater Robotics**
- 🛰️ **Multi-Agent Intelligence**
- 🗺️ **Trajectory & Path Planning**
- 📡 **Software Defined Radio**
- 📶 **Underwater Communication**

My current research focuses on intelligent decision-making for
**Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs)**, particularly in:

- Multi-AUV trajectory planning
- Autonomous navigation
- Area coverage
- Collision avoidance
- Cooperative multi-agent coordination
- Bathymetry-aware planning
- Communication-aware autonomy
- Realistic robotic simulation

The broader objective is to connect **AI algorithms, autonomous robots,
simulation environments and communication systems** toward deployable
intelligent underwater systems.

## 🤖 Multi-Agent Autonomous Intelligence

> Investigating how multiple autonomous agents can learn, coordinate
> and make decisions collectively.

### Research Topics

- Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning
- Deep Reinforcement Learning
- Cooperative autonomous agents
- Centralized critics
- Decentralized actors
- Experience replay
- Multi-agent interaction
- Goal coordination
- Mission-level decision making

<details>
<summary><b>🧠 Why Multi-Agent Intelligence?</b></summary>

<br/>

A single autonomous vehicle may perform one task efficiently.

A coordinated group of autonomous vehicles can potentially:

- Cover larger regions
- Complete missions faster
- Share workload
- Improve robustness
- Coordinate navigation
- Avoid inter-agent collisions
- Adapt to mission changes

This makes multi-agent intelligence particularly relevant for
large-scale underwater missions.

</details>
